# NTP Groups — Chrome Web Store Listing

## Title
NTP Groups

## Summary
Organize favorite sites into clean groups on your New Tab page while keeping your browser's default search provider.

## Detailed description
NTP Groups replaces the New Tab page with a compact launcher built around grouped shortcuts.

Keep frequently used sites at the top level, place related sites into groups, and open a group without leaving the New Tab page. Sites and groups can be added, edited, moved, reordered, or removed directly from the interface.

Key features:

- Group favorite sites into compact folders.
- Preview grouped sites directly from the New Tab page.
- Drag and reorder shortcuts.
- Move sites between the root and groups.
- Use your browser's current default search provider through the official browser search API.
- Store shortcut titles, URLs, layout, and preferences locally in extension storage.
- No ads, analytics, trackers, remote scripts, or developer-operated backend.

Privacy note: NTP Groups handles the shortcut titles, URLs, layout, and preferences you choose to save. This information is stored locally in your browser and is not transmitted to the developer. Search text is handed to the browser's search API and is processed by the default search provider selected in your browser.

NTP Groups has one purpose: making the New Tab page a cleaner, faster place to organize and open your favorite sites.
